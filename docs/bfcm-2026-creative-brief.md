# BFCM 2026 bonus-offer artwork brief

## Recommended output

- Desktop: 2400 × 800 px, art weighted to the right so live HTML copy can sit on the left.
- Mobile: 1080 × 1350 px, centered stack with clear negative space at the top and bottom.
- Generate artwork only. Do not bake offer copy, prices, logos, buttons, or legal text into the image.
- Export WebP plus a lossless PNG master. Keep each under 500 KB after optimization when possible.

## Image-generation prompt

Create a clean, premium Black Friday / Cyber Monday promotional product illustration for Konnected, a modern smart-home hardware brand. Use the supplied product photos as strict visual references and preserve each product’s real shape, proportions, connectors, cable direction, and white or black finish. Arrange the products as three ascending bonus groups, suggesting $150, $200, and $250 reward levels without rendering any text or currency symbols. Show a white magnetic contact sensor and magnetic standoffs in the first group, a compact indoor siren and wired 12V-to-5V USB converter in the second group, and a premium white siren, white motion sensor, and USB-C PD converter in the third group. Keep the scene light, airy, and trustworthy with a white-to-pale-blue gradient, subtle concentric circuit-ring arcs, a restrained Konnected blue and electric-cyan glow, and small circuit-green highlight accents. Use realistic studio lighting, soft midnight-blue tinted shadows, crisp product cutouts, and generous negative space. No people, houses, shopping bags, gift boxes, confetti, snow, Christmas motifs, dark background, black-and-gold luxury styling, red sale bursts, fake screens, altered labels, illegible text, extra cables, duplicated products, or invented hardware. Photorealistic commercial e-commerce composition, polished but not flashy, accessible contrast, product-first, 3:1 panoramic layout.

For the mobile variant, keep the same visual language and product fidelity but rearrange the three groups into a compact vertical progression with safe cropping at 4:5.
